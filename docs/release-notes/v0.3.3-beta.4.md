# Dimble 0.3.3-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.3.3, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Added:

- Each display can now show hidden icons its own way. In Settings › Displays, every display that hides icons gets a "Reveal in" picker. Your monitor can reveal in the menu bar while your MacBook uses the panel or a row (#88, thanks @wyaqoob). Displays left on "Same as Panel tab" follow the choice in Settings › Panel.

Fixed:

- After closing the lid with external displays attached and opening it again, the chevron could go missing until Dimble relaunched. Dimble now checks the bar when your Mac wakes or the displays change, and puts the chevron back if it's gone (#26, thanks @cpierce).
