# Dimble 0.3.3-beta.1

First beta of 0.3.3, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Changed:

- In Settings › Menu Bar › Show when it matters, an icon that sits in Visible now has a "Move to Hidden" link. It moves the icon and turns its rule on. Before, the switch was just greyed out (#92).

Fixed:

- With the Clock hidden, clicking Control Center or the spot where the clock used to be no longer flashes your hidden icons on the bar. Those clicks go straight to Control Center now (#93, thanks @punchmac).
- ⌥-click on the clock turns Do Not Disturb on and off again, the way it does without Dimble. It did nothing while icons were hidden (#92, thanks @juanfcocontreras).
