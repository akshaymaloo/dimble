# Dimble 0.3.2-beta.8

> **Pelmet is now Dimble.** Pliss turned out to be one letter off from something else (#91), so I decided to rename it one more time before the stable release. It's the same app, and your settings, permissions and launch at login carry over. On its first launch after the update, the app renames itself to Dimble.app and opens again.
>
> If anything looks off after the rename, please tell me on GitHub.

Eighth beta of 0.3.2, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Changed:

- The app and its Settings say Dimble in all ten languages. About keeps the small "formerly Pelmet" line.
- The app icon is a D now, same dots.
- Logs are now in ~/Library/Logs/Dimble/dimble.log, and your old logs move there too.
- The repo is now github.com/fif7y/dimble, and old GitHub links keep working.

Fixed:

- Changing "Show hidden icons in" while the hidden icons are out now puts them away first, and the next click opens them in the new place. Before, an open row could turn into a grid, or the icons stayed in the menu bar (#26, thanks @cpierce).
