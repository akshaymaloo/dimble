# Dimble 0.3.3-beta.3

Third beta of 0.3.3, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Changed:

- Clicking Dimble's Siri now opens Type to Siri, like a click on Apple's own Siri icon, and a second click closes it. For voice, right-click it and pick "Talk to Siri" (#83, thanks @swhitlow).

Fixed:

- ⌥-click on the clock turns Do Not Disturb on and off with "Clicking the clock opens Notification Center" turned off too (#92, thanks @juanfcocontreras).
- Applying a change in Settings › Menu Bar no longer leaves blank space right of the chevron while it moves your icons. Camera and Media each took a slot there whenever they were idle.
