# Dimble 0.3.3-beta.8

Eighth beta of 0.3.3, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Fixed:

- The Stop button of a screen recording (⇧⌘5) now shows while Dimble hides your icons. It's in Visible, right of the chevron. Before, you only saw it with Always Hidden open, or after quitting Dimble.
- When one of your displays shows all its icons, the chevron on your other displays stays in sight. A new icon to its right, like Camera & Mic or the Stop button, used to hide it.
