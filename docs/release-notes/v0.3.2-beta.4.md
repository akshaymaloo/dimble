# Pelmet 0.3.2-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.3.2, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Fixed:

- On a crowded bar, the panel and Row show your icons instead of a row of arrows. Icons macOS had tucked behind its « got a picture of the « itself. Now they're skipped, and the ones that can't get room in the bar show their app icon (#81, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif, and #26, thanks @OiCkilL).
- With more than one display, a right-click on Pelmet's chevron opens its menu on the display you clicked. It used to open where the panel was last open (#26, thanks @cpierce).
- Icons that sit on a capsule in the menu bar, like Velja's and Herd's, show without it in the panel. On a coloured wallpaper the capsule came out as a box around the icon.
- "Always Hidden" fits on its fold tile, the name is a size under the others.
- The Search tab in Settings gets a New chip, gone once you open it.
