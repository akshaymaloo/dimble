# Pelmet 0.3.2-beta.6

> **The panel works on every display now.** It opens on the display you click or hover, right under that display's bar. A tile there opens its icon's menu on the same bar. The rest is mostly fixes from a full review of 0.3.2 before it goes stable.
>
> If you run Pelmet with more than one display, I'd love to hear how it does there.

Sixth beta of 0.3.2, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

New:

- The panel opens on the display you clicked or hovered, under an external display's taller bar too. A shortcut opens it on the display under the pointer (#26, thanks @cpierce).
- A tile pressed in the panel on another display opens the icon's menu on that display's bar (#26, thanks @cpierce).
- Turn an app's own menu bar icon off and its tile leaves the panel and the row. It's back when you turn the icon on again. CleanShot kept its tile with its icon off (#26, thanks @cpierce).
- Bluetooth, Display, Focus, Now Playing and the user menu show their own mark while their tile waits for a picture, instead of a dashed box (#26, thanks @cpierce).

Fixed:

- Panel tiles keep their picture while Pelmet takes a new one. They turned into colour app icons for a second every 15 minutes (#81, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif).
- The line under every panel tile on some displays should be gone. Pelmet now films only as tall as the bar itself (#81, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif).
- A panel or popover that hangs a couple of points into the bar (Snib's does) no longer draws a line under a tile.
- An icon you drop into Hidden or Always Hidden in Settings crosses the arrow on its own a few seconds later, instead of waiting for Apply.
- The Apply button keeps its count through a reveal or a drop. A reorder in a hidden section counts once, and an icon dragged out and back to its own spot doesn't count at all.
- An icon you ⌘-drag stays where you dropped it, past one of Pelmet's own icons or on another display's bar.
- Clicks open menus again after an Apply. Pelmet could leave ⌘ held after its last drag.
- Hide again offers half a second again, so a 0.5s delay doesn't snap to 1 second anymore.
- If you upgraded with Focus showing, it keeps coming out while a Focus is on. Wi-Fi's grace period starts over after sleep.
- Typing into the command bar or a Settings field stays there with a hover panel open, and a held key never sticks.
- Recording a shortcut stays in its field, and an alias keeps what you type.
- A second ⌥⌘N closes Notification Center for good.
- A locked Mac no longer reads the menu bar every 2 seconds all night.
