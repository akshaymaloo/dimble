# Pliss 0.3.2-beta.7

> **Pelmet is now Pliss.** Another macOS menu bar app had the name Pelmet first, so I decided to rename ours before the stable release. It's the same app, and your settings, permissions and launch at login carry over. On its first launch after the update, the app renames itself from Pelmet.app to Pliss.app and opens again.
>
> If anything looks off after the rename, please tell me on GitHub.

Seventh beta of 0.3.2, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases").

Changed:

- The app and its Settings say Pliss in all ten languages. About keeps a small "formerly Pelmet" line.
- Logs are now in ~/Library/Logs/Pliss/pliss.log, and your old Pelmet logs move there too.
- Updates come from a new feed address. Nothing to do on your side.

New:

- Time Machine, VPN, AirDrop and Siri tiles show their own mark while they wait for a picture, instead of a dashed circle (#26, thanks @cpierce).

Fixed:

- Clicking the clock should open Notification Center again on Macs where the app thought it was already open (#81, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif).
- Panel tile pictures should be sharp on a non-Retina display. Thin lines smeared into a grey edge (#81, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif).
- Settings opens a little taller, so About fits without a scroll.
